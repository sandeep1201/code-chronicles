# Extended anti-AI pattern reference (blogs)

Use when a deep pass is needed. Condensed from [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) and [Humanizer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blader/humanizer/refs/heads/main/SKILL.md).

## Significance / legacy inflation

Words/phrases to flag: stands as, serves as, testament, reminder, vital/significant/crucial/pivotal, underscores/highlights importance, reflects broader, symbolizing, contributing to, setting the stage, marking/shaping, represents a shift, turning point, evolving landscape, focal point, deeply rooted.

## Notability / media flex

Vague "featured in many outlets" lists without a point. Prefer one specific citation and what was said.

## Promotional / adjective soup

boasts, vibrant, rich (figurative), enhancing its, exemplifies, natural beauty, nestled, in the heart of, renowned, must-visit.

## Outline clichés

Despite its success... faces challenges... Despite these challenges... continues to thrive... Future outlook. Replace with specific facts, dates, or trade-offs.

## More AI-high-frequency words

delve, fostering, garner, showcase (verb), interplay, intricacies, enduring, enhance (when empty), furthermore (sometimes fine—don't ban blindly; notice density).

## False ranges

"From X to Y, from A to B" when X/Y/A/B are not on one axis. List topics plainly instead.

## Filler → shorter

- In order to → To
- Due to the fact that → Because
- At this point in time → Now
- It is important to note that → (delete or start with the fact)
- has the ability to → can

## Hedging stacks

could potentially possibly might → one level of uncertainty or a clear claim.

## Generic upbeat endings

The future looks bright; exciting times ahead; journey toward excellence → specific next step, plan, or honest open question.

## Collaborative / assistant tone pasted as content

Certainly!, You're absolutely right!, Would you like me to..., Here's an overview — strip from published posts.

## Title case in markdown headings

Prefer sentence case for H2/H3 unless the site standard is otherwise: `## Key takeaways` not `## Key Takeaways` — **Code Chronicles** uses title case in some templates; follow the existing post you're editing or `BLOG_WRITING_PROMPT.md` for consistency.

## Hyphenated pairs

AI text hyphenates uniformly (cross-functional, data-driven, end-to-end). Natural prose varies; don't hyphenate every compound on autopilot.

## Negative parallelisms

Three AI variants to use sparingly: "Not only X, but also Y" / "It's not just X, it's Y"; "Not X, but Y"; "X rather than Y". Often one plain clause says it better.

## Inline-header vertical lists

AI tell: a list where every item is `**Bold header:** description sentence.` (or emoji/dash markers with a bolded lead-in). Prefer prose, or keep this shape only for true definition lists — not for every list.

## Copula / verb swaps (avoidance of "is/are")

serves as, stands as, functions as, operates as, represents, boasts, features, offers, maintains, refers to → is / are / has. Also watch "began his career as" / "ventured into" where "was" is meant.

## Signs of human writing (aim for these)

- Plain is/has ("there is a", "it has a").
- Blunt verbs vs stiff synonyms: wrote/authored, used/utilized, died/passed away, moved/relocated, tried/attempted.
- Superlatives when true: "the first", "one of the best".
- Hedges/intensifiers: very, perhaps, tends to.
- Human wordiness in moderation: in order to, the fact that, as a result of.

## Ineffective indicators (don't treat as AI)

Perfect grammar; formal/academic/"fancy" prose; a lone transition word; mixed casual+technical register; presence of citations; correct or bizarre wikitext/markup. None of these alone mean AI — density and clustering of the real tells is what matters.
